HomeOps — Home Operations Toolkit

A work in progress focused on reliable, secure, and recoverable home infrastructure.

HomeOps is a project exploring how to make self-hosted infrastructure easier to understand, monitor, maintain, and recover — without turning home lab management into a second job.

The goal is a practical operations toolkit that prioritizes system health, meaningful alerts, repeatable procedures, and safe automation.

🎯 Project Goals

* Visibility: Make system health and important issues easy to understand at a glance.
* Reliability: Develop repeatable checks and maintenance procedures.
* Recoverability: Document backup verification and recovery workflows.
* Automation: Reduce repetitive work while preserving human control over significant changes.
* Security: Favor private remote access, least exposure, and deliberate change management.
* Simplicity: Prefer low-maintenance solutions and useful outcomes over unnecessary complexity.

🛠️ Areas of Exploration

The project is being developed around several practical areas:

Area	Direction
Health monitoring	Clear status reporting and meaningful alerts
Operations	Repeatable audits and maintenance procedures
Backup and recovery	Verification workflows and recovery documentation
Automation	Small, controlled, and reversible tasks
Documentation	Useful runbooks, architecture notes, and decision records
Remote administration	Security-conscious management of self-hosted systems

These are project goals and areas of development, not a claim that every capability is already implemented.

🔐 Engineering Principles

* Read-only investigation by default.
* Human approval before significant changes.
* Small, reversible changes wherever practical.
* Verification before and after approved changes.
* No unnecessary public exposure of infrastructure.
* No secrets or private operational details in public materials.
* Clear separation between verified results, assumptions, recommendations, and open questions.

📦 What to Expect Here

As the project develops, this repository may contain sanitized documentation, reusable scripts, example reports, and practical procedures for home infrastructure operations.

Private configuration, credentials, internal network details, and environment-specific operational records will remain outside the public repository.

🚧 Status

Early development — scope and components are evolving.

The aim is not to build the biggest home lab. It is to make the systems you already operate more understandable, dependable, and recoverable.
